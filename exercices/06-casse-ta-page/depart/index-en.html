Le Pigala, a neighbourhood brasserie in Montreuil

Restaurant and bar in Montreuil, opened this summer at 12 rue des Houblonnières, a three-minute walk from Croix de Chavaux metro station. Eat in or take away.

Call +33 1 23 45 67 89

The spirit of the place

“We're only waiting for you to take the first step; the rest is up to us.”

Push the door open for a lunch with colleagues, a pizza in Montreuil to share with friends, a drink on the terrace after work or a Sunday brunch. At Le Pigala, we serve food and drink without ceremony, and we take the time to welcome you.

  • Salads, burgers, pizzas and tapas
  • Terrace happy hour
  • Karaoke in Montreuil on Thursdays
  • Game nights

Opening hours, happy hour and karaoke

Opening hours

  • Tuesday to Saturday: 11:30 am to midnight
  • Sunday: 11 am to 4 pm, it's brunch
  • Closed on Monday

Happy hour on the terrace

From 5 pm to 7 pm, Tuesday to Friday. A bar in Montreuil to end the day in the sun, a drink in hand.

Karaoke on Thursdays

From 8 pm to 11 pm, every Thursday. Come and sing, or simply listen: the neighbourhood karaoke in Montreuil is open to all voices.
